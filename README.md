## What I build

Live products, not prototypes. Each one is a place I had to make the calls that actually matter in AI products: what the model may draft, what must stay deterministic, and what the user still owns.

### [Ogeegee](https://ogeegee.com) · Career OS
A private workspace for a serious job search. Paste a job description, get a grounded apply / refer / skip decision, then carry that decision through pipeline, attention, and interview prep.

Invite-only. Case study: [`ogeegee`](https://github.com/farlaw/ogeegee)

### [PM Mock Buddy](https://pmmockbuddy.com)
An AI mock-interview coach for product managers. Voice or text answers, a follow-up, then structured feedback you can actually iterate on. Live on the [web](https://pmmockbuddy.com) and the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756758029).

Case study: [`pmmockbuddy-public`](https://github.com/farlaw/pmmockbuddy-public)

### [Playlistour](https://playlistour.com)
Concert discovery from music you already listen to. Connect YouTube Music or Spotify, pick a city, and see upcoming shows with a match score and a plain-language “why.” Live on the [web](https://playlistour.com) and the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/playlistour/id6759816667).

Case study: [`playlistour-public`](https://github.com/farlaw/playlistour-public)
